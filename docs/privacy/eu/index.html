Hwatu Solitaire Privacy Notice for the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This notice applies to users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the Swiss FADP. It adds to our general privacy policy.

1. Who is responsible

Hwatu Solitaire (package com.kimhanwol.hwatusolitaire, the "App") is made by the individual developer KimHanWol (the "Developer"), based in the Republic of Korea. Contact: linkingmain@gmail.com.

2. What the Developer does not collect

The App has no account or sign-in and the Developer runs no user database. The Developer does not receive your name, email, contacts, precise location or photos. Your game progress and settings stay on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Advertising data processed through Google AdMob

The App shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When you use the App, the following may be processed by Google:

Advertising ID, cookies or similar identifiers stored on the device Storing or accessing information on your device; personalised ads and ad measurement Your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a))

Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your consent and how to change it

On first launch the App shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). Ads load according to your choice. You can change or withdraw consent at any time in the App under Settings → Ad privacy options. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it, and you can keep playing the App. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

5. International transfers

Data processed by Google may be transferred to and stored in countries outside your own, including the United States. Google relies on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers. The Developer is based in the Republic of Korea but does not receive this data.

6. Retention

The Developer stores no personal data. Data on your device stays until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Google keeps advertising data for the periods set out in its own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, to data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent. Because the Developer holds no personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best made to Google, through My Ad Center and your Google account. You can still contact the Developer at the address above, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country (up to 16 in parts of the EEA), and the Developer does not knowingly collect their personal data.

9. Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
